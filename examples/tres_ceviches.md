
## Ceviche

SOURCE: https://cuisinewithme.com/mixed-seafood-ceviche/#recipe
SOURCE: https://pinchandswirl.com/scallop-ceviche/#wprm-recipe-container-42234

Ingredients:

 - 1/4 pounds White fish cod, tilapia or other
 - 1/4 pound Scallops
 - 1/4 pound Shrimp peeled and deveined

 - 1/2 cup juice + zest: lime
 - 1/2 cup juice + zest: lemon
 - 1/2 cup juice + zest: tangerine
 - 1/4 cup cider vinegar

 - 1/2 cup chopped shallots - or white/yellow onion
 - 1 small serrano pepper, thin slices
 - 1 small tomatillo, chuncked
 - 1 small heirloom tomato, chunked
 - 1 small mango, minced finely
 - 1 Tbsp sliced black olives

 - 1/4 cup cilantro, choppd
 - 1/2 tsp powered coriander
 - 1 tsp sea salt
 - 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper

Instructions:

  + Rinse and pat dry the fish, shrimp and scallops. 
  + Cut all seafood into small pieces (about 1/4 inch).
  + Place in a glass bowl.
  + Add peppers, shallots, tomatos, mango.
  + Cover completely with citrus juice, vinegar, and salt.

  + Cover and refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours, stirring occasionally -- should be no remaining translucent spots in the fish.
  + When ready to serve, add cilantro.


Ideas:

 - Drain liquid, mix with veggie consommé and tequila (like a shrub), then serve as a soup
 - Use deep-fried corn+ube crêpes in place of tortilla chips
