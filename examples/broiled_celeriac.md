
# Broiled Celeriac Steak

https://www.allrecipes.com/roasted-celeriac-reubens-recipe-8606828
https://chefsecretsource.com/how-to-roast-celeriac/
https://samcookskindness.com/instant-pot-celery-root-puree/
https://akispetretzikis.com/en/recipe/6104/steak-selinorizas-sth-chytra-tachythtas

- 2x celeriac root (~400 g)

* wash celeriac roots under cold water to remove any dirt
* trim off top and bottom ends, to create a stable base for peeling
* stand on flat end and carefully peel away thick skin
* support with flatware knives to make Hasselback cuts
* soak for 15-20 min in water + vinegar (acidulated water, to avoid browning)

- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- bay leaf
- smashed garlic clove
- 1 Tbs olive oil
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp dried cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup fish buillion
- 1/2 cup maple syrup

* instant pot: pressure cook on high for 12 min, then allow 15 min for natural release
* transfer liquid to a sauté pan and reduce to a thick sauce

- drizzle celeriac with olive oil and maple syrup

* broil at 200°C, to get caramelization and tender interior

- 1 Tbs minced tarragon leaves

* spoon reduced sauce over celeriac root
* garnish with tarragon


good served over white rice, with fish or chicken
