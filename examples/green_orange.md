
# Green Orange Sauce

A lightly sweet-sour sauce, which looks green and tastes orange!

  POST: "https://www.instagram.com/p/DWNAMmRktES/"

- 225 g fresh celery
- 1 clove garlic
- 5 g oil
- 5 g sea salt
- 1 g agar agar powder
- 15 ml warm water
- 50 ml white wine, vermouth, Lillet, etc.
- 150 ml tangerine gastrique (or other acidic fruit)
- 30 g fresh greens (spinach, miners lettuce, cress, etc.)
- 18 g toasted sesame seeds

- cutting board
- small cup
- tiny whisk
- sauté pan
- nutribullet blender
- food mill
- half-pint glass jar

cup:
  - add agar gar, water
  - mix with small whisk

board:
 - chop the celery
 - crush and mince the garlic

pan:
 - transfer from board
 - add oil, salt
 - sauté until celery begins to brown
 - transfer from cup
 - add white wine
 - stir to deglaze

cutting board:
 - chop the greens

nutribullet:
 - transfer from pan
 - transfer from board
 - add gastrique
 - blend into a fine purée

food mill:
 - transfer from nutribullet
 - pass purée through mill to filter out fibrous material (can be used for stocks)

jar:
 - transfer from mill
 - add sesame seeds
 - stir
