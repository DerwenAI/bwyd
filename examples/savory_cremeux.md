# Savory Crémeux
> A savory crémeux is a smooth, spoonable custard, usually made like a pastry crème but flavored with ingredients such as cheese, herbs, miso, mushroom, or roasted garlic. It’s set gently with gelatin, then blended until silky and chilled until firm enough to pipe or quenelle.

## Basic method
1. Warm cream and/or milk with your savory flavor base.
2. Whisk egg yolks with a little salt and, if needed, a small amount of sugar to help balance the flavor.
3. Temper the yolks with the hot dairy, then cook gently to about 80-83° C, stirring constantly until it lightly thickens.
4. Strain, add bloomed gelatin, then emulsify with butter or cheese for extra richness.
5. Blend smooth, cover the surface, and chill at least 6 hours or overnight.

## Easy savory formula
For a small batch:

- 250 g milk
- 150 g cream
- 4 egg yolks
- 3 g gelatin, bloomed in cold water
- 40–80 g savory ingredient, such as parmesan, goat cheese, white miso, roasted cauliflower puree, or mushroom duxelles
- salt and pepper to taste

1. Heat the milk, cream, and flavoring, then proceed with tempering and cooking as above.
2. If the savory ingredient is already salty, add less salt.

## Flavor ideas
* Parmesan and black pepper
* Goat cheese and chive
* Roasted garlic and thyme
* White miso and sesame
* Mushroom and shallot
* Herb and cream cheese, using a formula similar to savory cheese-based crémeux

## Texture tips
A proper crémeux should be glossy, smooth, and stable enough to hold shape without feeling like a firm pudding.

Avoid boiling after the yolks go in, and use an immersion blender briefly to make the emulsion finer without adding air.

If it seems too loose, add a little more gelatin next time; if it seems too firm, reduce gelatin slightly.

## Serving ideas
Use savory crémeux as a base for plated vegetables, a tart filling, a canapé spread, or a sauce component under fish or chicken.

It works especially well with crunchy toppings, herbs, pickles, or crispy breadcrumbs for contrast.
