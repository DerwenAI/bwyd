
# perfect dry pasta ratio

1 l water
10 g salt
100 g dry pasta

* use a big pot (avoids starch build-up)
* bring water to a boil first
* never rinse after draining
* reuse the pasta water to help emulsify sauces

* cook no longer than 70% of the time on the package
* cook until the pasta floats/hydrates and tastes appropriately (al dente)
* finish cooking in the sauce
