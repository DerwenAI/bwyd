
# Green Oil

Sources:
 - <https://food52.com/recipes/76835-emerald-green-herb-oil>
 - <https://simplybeyondherbs.com/green-herb-oil/>
 - <https://lcartefood.com/easy-green-herb-oil-with-parsley-chef-style-recipe/>

Ingredients:
 - 50 g fresh greens (use stems for soft greens, e.g., parsley)
 - 100 ml oil

Steps:
ratio: 1 greens : 2 oil

measure and chill the oil

create an ice bath in a mixing bowl, using just enough water to cover the ice

boil 1 liter water, lightly salted

blanch herbs:
wash the fresh herbs, then shake off excess water

add herbs to the boiling water
let cook for 10-20 seconds, until they wilt and darken slightly in color

transfer herbs to the ice bath and let cool 5 minutes

remove herbs and squeeze out as much excess water as possible
pat dry on a paper towel

blitz herbs with oil for 1 minute
pause and open lid to release steam
blitz for 1-2 minutes, until completely smooth and well combined

transfer mixture to a small sauce pan and bring to a light simmer

line a strainer with cheesecloth, atop a mixing bowl

transfer herbs/oil mixture into the strainer

don't force mixture through the strainer; let the oil drain by gravity for 1-2 hours

store green oil in a squeeze bottle in the fridge for 5 days, or until the color begins to fade
