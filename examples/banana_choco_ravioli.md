
# Banana Chocolate Ravioli

Fresh banana pasta, stuffed with Grand Marnier chocolate ganache, then sautéed in a hot buttered rum flambé made with Bacardi 151, and served with a lemon mascarpone mousse.

## pasta sheets

  - 2 cup "00" pasta flour
  - 1/3 cup sugar (caster sugar, blitz to reduce crystal size)
  - 1 cup Banana pulp (about 1.5 large bananas)
  - 1 egg
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract

blend thoroughly in a large mixing bowl
wrap and allow to rest in frigo at least 30 min
knead 10 min to develop gluten, adding "00" flour to reduce moisture/stickiness
roll out 10 cm wide pasta sheets, setting 4 or thinner

yields: enough pasta for approx 30 ravioli


## ganache

 - 4 oz dark chocolate
 - 1 Tbsp Grand Marnier
 - 1.5 Tbsp butter
 - 1/2 cup heavy cream
 - 1 tsp orange zest

yields: enough for approx 25 ravioli


## ravioli

- use a melon baller to scoop ganache balls (5 g each)
- roll each ball lightly in flour
- place in freezer

- align 10 cm wide pasta sheet on a pastry mat
- space the ganache balls evenly
- brush seams of pasta with cold water
- fold over, press, and cut ravioli
- layer on floured parchment sheets
- store up to 3 days in frigo


## hot buttered rum sauce

 - 2 tsp packed brown sugar
 - 1 Tbs unsalted butter
 - 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
 - 1 dash ground cinnamon
 - 1 dash ground nutmeg
 - 1 dash ground allspice
 - 2 oz rum
 - 4 oz hot water


## lemon mascarpone gelato

 - 200 g mascarpone
 - gelato base
 - juice + zest of a medium lemon
 - pinch salt

alternatively, reserve 1/4 and whip into a mousse, which at serving can be piped atop the gelato

## cooking

- place ravioli into lightly boiling water
- flip gently to get even cooking on both sides
- boil until the ravioli float to top (4 min)

- remove, drip-dry, place into sauté pan
- optional: add banana slices (can be too much banana flavor for some)
- drizzle Grand Marnier over ravioli
- sauté ravioli in hot buttered rum sauce (3 min)
- transfer to serving dish, drizzle with Bacardi 151
- flambé until alcohol is removed (3 min)
- serve ravioli plus some sauce (no flame)
- add gelato/mousse in a half orange peel, topped with orange tuile and orange pearls
- garnish with finger lime "caviar" if available
